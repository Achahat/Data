Blow-off Short — F&O shares onlyMonthly + 2-day bars
Monthly RSI crosses the level → P0. Journey must never close a month below the floor (else reset, wait for a fresh cross). 2-Day RSI crosses its level at price P → short when price reaches P × (1+rise%), only if that level ≥ ×P0. Cover on first monthly close below the floor, or at +stop% against you. Universe: current F&O stocks.
Index Futures — RSI + Stoch reversal (long)Daily bars
Buys one lot of the index future when the daily RSI and Stochastic %K of the index are both oversold, and exits when either turns. Positions roll to the next month a few days before expiry — the roll cost is charged to the trade.
Spread paid at each roll
Buy Grid — add a lot every N points down, release one every N points up
Long-only grid on round levels (24,600 · 24,500 · …). Each line crossed downwards buys a lot; each line crossed upwards releases the lot resting one step below. Gaps are priced honestly: if the day opens past several lines, every one of those orders fills at the open, so a 500-point gap up sells five lots there and pays far more than one step. It never goes net short.
Spread paid at each roll
Short Grid — sell a lot every N points up, cover one every N points down
Short-only grid — the mirror of the buy grid. Each line crossed upwards sells a lot; each line crossed downwards covers the short resting one step above. Gap opens fill at the open, not at the line, so a big gap down covers several shorts at once for more than a step each. It never goes net long.
Spread received at each roll
RSI + Stoch on shares — the futures rule, run across a market-cap bandMonthly bars
Monthly bars, every listed share — no shortlist. A share is bought when its monthly RSI and Stoch %K are both oversold and its market cap sits inside your band on that month's close; it is sold when either turns. The band is checked on the entry month itself, so a share only counts if it really was that size at the time. A full pass reads about 3,000 files, so give it a few minutes.
Rollover — what carrying a position to the next month costs
Three trading days before each expiry, this compares the near month with the next month. A positive spread is what a long pays to carry forward (and what a short receives); a negative spread is the reverse.
Nifty-Timed Short — the index calls the timing, F&O stocks take the positionDaily bars
Every time the Nifty 50 daily RSI crosses up through your level, the weakest F&O stocks by daily RSI are shorted at that day's close. The basket is covered on the first day the Nifty daily Stoch %K falls to your cover level — or after your maximum holding period, whichever comes first. Leave the day limit blank to wait for the Stoch signal however long it takes. One basket at a time.
